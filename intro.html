Pedro Leite — Research Hub#

MSc in Electrical Engineering (COPPE/UFRJ), Audio Processing Group (GPA) — speech synthesis, voice datasets and accent modelling for Brazilian Portuguese.

Brazilian Portuguese is among the most widely spoken languages in the world and one of the worst served by open speech data. Most of the work collected here follows from that: record the data properly, release it, then write down what it actually takes to train on it. This portal gathers the dissertation, the papers, the corpora we published, and the audio examples that go with them — because for speech work, listening is the argument.

Dissertation#

COPPE/UFRJ · October 2024 · Advisor: Luiz Wagner Pereira Biscainho

Increasing the Robustness of Brazilian Portuguese Voice Synthesis

What it takes to make Brazilian Portuguese TTS good enough to go on air — from recording 40 hours of studio speech, through the phoneme-level fixes for the pronunciations the models keep getting wrong, to prosody, emotion and speaker-identity controls on top.

Datasets#

Two professionally recorded, gender-balanced single-speaker corpora for Brazilian Portuguese TTS — same sentences, same protocol, one male and one female voice. Both are free to download.

~20 h 10,333 segments

🎙️ Neutral Speech — Male

Single male speaker, neutral emotion, studio-recorded at 24-bit/96 kHz. Introduced at PROPOR 2022.

Download on Kaggle →

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mediatechlab/gneutralspeech

~20 h 10,333 segments

🎙️ Neutral Speech — Female

Single female speaker, same sentences and same protocol as the male corpus. Introduced at SBrT 2023.

Download on Kaggle →

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mediatechlab/g-neutral-speech-female
